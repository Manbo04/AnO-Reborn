help_outlineHow a Bond works
  1. You list a Bond for an amount (the principal), a daily interest rate and a term in days.
  2. An investor buys it and the principal lands in your treasury straight away.
  3. Every day of the term you pay interest on the full principal to the investor — taken from your treasury automatically.
  4. At the end of the term you pay the principal back in one go (or bit by bit each day if you tick auto-escrow).

Example: a $1,000,000 Bond at 1%/day for 10 days gives you $1,000,000 now, costs you $10,000 every day, and you repay the $1,000,000 on day 10 — $1,100,000 paid back in total. Buying a Bond is the same deal from the other side.

Defaults: missing too many interest payments, or not covering the principal at maturity, defaults the Bond — the game keeps collecting what you still owe, you can't issue a new Bond for a while, and your national currency's price is capped on the Currency Exchange until it's paid.
