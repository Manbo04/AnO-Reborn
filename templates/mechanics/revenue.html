paid Tax Income Formula

calculate Base Calculation

Working-age citizens pay taxes each hour; elderly pay 45% of the rate and children pay nothing. The base rate is modified by various factors.

Base Tax Rate$0.75 per working-age citizen per hour (elderly $0.34, children $0)
Example (1M working-age)$750,000 per hour base
Example (10M working-age)$7,500,000 per hour base

tune Modifiers

Consumer Goods SuppliedUp to +50% taxes, per province, in proportion to how much of its consumer goods need your retail buildings there can deliver (goods shipped in from other provinces count half)
No RationsUp to -30% taxes, scaled to how short your rations are (full -30% only with none reaching your people)
Land Slots+2% per land slot beyond the first, per province (max +100% at 51 land)
trending_up Productivity & Production

speed How Productivity Works

Productivity affects all resource production from infrastructure.

Formula+0.9% output per % productivity above 50% (below 50% reduces output)
At 50% (baseline)100% normal production
At 75%+22.5% production bonus
At 100%+45% production bonus

hub Specialisation & Vertical Integration

stacked_bar_chart Specialisation (economies of scale)

Mines, farms, lumber mills, processing buildings and Industrial Districts produce more the bigger the share of a province's slots they fill. Returns diminish: the first buildings count most.

Formula+15% × (1 − e−4 × share)
10% of slotsabout +5% output
25% of slotsabout +9.5% output
50% of slotsabout +13% output
Maximum+15% output

link Vertical Integration

Steel mills, refineries and factories produce more when the same province produces its own inputs. The bonus scales with how self-sufficient that province is in the scarcest input: what its mines really produce per hour (productivity, national projects, specialisation and workforce included) compared with what those buildings consume.

Fully self-sufficient+10% output
Half self-sufficient+5% output
Imports everythingNo bonus
Where to see itEach building card on the province page

lightbulb Tips to Maximize Income

  • Keep rations supplied to avoid the -30% penalty
  • Match consumer goods to population (about 1 per 80k; elderly need more) and keep retail buildings in every province
  • Build productivity infrastructure early
  • Buy land slots for +2% tax bonus each
  • Sell surplus resources on the market
