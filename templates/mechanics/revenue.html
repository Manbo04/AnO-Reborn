stacked_bar_chart Specialisation (economies of scale)

Mines, farms, lumber mills, processing buildings and Industrial Districts produce more the bigger the share of a province's slots they fill. Returns diminish: the first buildings count most.

Formula+15% × (1 − e−4 × share)
10% of slotsabout +5% output
25% of slotsabout +9.5% output
50% of slotsabout +13% output
Maximum+15% output

link Vertical Integration

Steel mills, refineries and factories produce more when your nation produces its own inputs. The bonus scales with how self-sufficient you are in the scarcest input (your mines' base output compared with what those buildings consume).

Fully self-sufficient+10% output
Half self-sufficient+5% output
Imports everythingNo bonus
Where to see itEach building card on the province page
