restaurant Rations (Food)

The most critical resource - without rations, your population cannot grow.

Consumption Rate~1 per 50,000 population/hour (children need 30% more, elderly 20% less)
Produced ByFarms (100 rations/hour)
Farm Bonus+3 rations per land slot
DistributionDistribution buildings must exist to move food; capacity is tiered by type, from 250k pop (Food Banks) up to 5M pop (Malls) — see table below.
Without RationsNo growth, -30% taxes

lightbulb Resource Management Tips

trending_up Maximizing Production

  • Build productivity infrastructure (schools) first
  • Productivity at 100% gives +45% resource output
  • Buy more land to boost farm output
  • Upgrades like Advanced Machinery increase yields

store Trading Resources

  • Check market prices before selling
  • Buy resources you don't produce efficiently
  • Some resources are more valuable than others
  • Bot offers provide baseline market prices
