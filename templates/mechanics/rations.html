info Rations Overview

description What Are Rations?

Rations are the most critical resource in the game. They represent food that your population consumes to survive and grow. Without sufficient rations, your population grows more slowly, people starve and your tax income suffers.

PurposeFeed your population
If SufficientPopulation grows
If InsufficientSlower growth, up to 0.25% of people starve per hour, up to -30% taxes (a nation with one province and 20 land or less is spared the tax penalty and starvation)

schedule When Do Rations Update?

Rations are processed hourly by two separate tasks:

Your rations will increase at :25 (farms produce), then decrease at :45 (population eats).

ProductionEvery hour at :25
ConsumptionEvery hour at :45

trending_up Rations Production

agriculture Farms

Farms and Fisheries are the ways to produce rations. Farm output depends on land owned; Fisheries do not scale with land.

Base Production100 rations/hour/farm
Land Bonus+3 rations/hour per land slot, for every farm in the province
Fisheries160 rations/hour each, $1,800/hour, no land bonus
Operating Cost$3,000/hour/farm
TimingEvery hour at :25

functions Production Formula

Rations = Farms × (100 + Land × 3) × Productivity multiplier

At 100% productivity: +45% bonus. Many farms in one province add a further specialisation bonus of up to +15%, and a shortage of workers lowers output. Farms only run if you can pay their $3,000/hour upkeep.

swap_vert Understanding Net Rations

calculate Net = Production − Consumption

The net rations shown on your country page is:

Net = (Farm and Fishery Production) − (Population Consumption) − (Soldier Upkeep)

Each soldier eats 1 ration per hour.

Positive NetYou're stockpiling rations ✓
Zero NetBreaking even
Negative NetRations decreasing ⚠️

lightbulb Tips for Positive Net

  • Build more farms (more production)
  • Acquire more land (higher per-farm output)
  • Increase productivity (universities, libraries)
  • Research Advanced Machinery upgrade
  • Buy rations from the market

local_shipping Distribution Limits

Rations only reach people through distribution buildings. From Mon 12 Oct 2026, 18:00 UTC every building serves a fixed number of people; anyone beyond your total capacity goes unfed, so big nations need more of them. Once your nation is above its comfortable population, each building serves less than its listed number.

Food Banks250,000 people each
Gas Stations1,000,000 people each
Distribution Centers1,500,000 people each
Farmers Markets2,000,000 people each
General Stores2,500,000 people each
Malls5,000,000 people each

Banked rations above your storage buffer (14 days of consumption plus a bonus per distribution building) lose 2% of the excess per hour. Each distribution building adds storage: Food Bank 333,333, Gas Station 1,333,333, Distribution Center 2,000,000, Farmers Market 2,666,667, General Store 3,333,333, Mall 6,666,667 rations.

help FAQ

sync_problem Why Are My Rations Going Down?

  • Check your net: Is it positive or negative?
  • Build more farms: Match consumption with production
  • Timing: Rations update at :25 (add) and :45 (subtract)
  • Refresh: Wait for both tasks to run before judging

warning Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting the per-province minimum consumption
  • Not accounting for low productivity provinces
  • Checking rations between :45 and :25 (only consumption visible)
  • Not having enough money to operate farms ($3k/farm/hr)
