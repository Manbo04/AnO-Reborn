rule War Types

You pick a war type when you declare war, and it stays fixed for that war. It changes what a winning attack does to the defender.

local_fire_department Raze

Maximizes damage to the enemy's buildings. No gold is looted.

Province damage10× normal. Each winning attack can destroy up to 25% of the buildings in one random enemy province
Gold lootedNo gold taken
Daily plunder after the warOnly 5% per day instead of 20% (see How a War Ends)

balance Sustained

The baseline option — no bonus and no penalty to province damage, no gold looted per attack.

Province damageNormal. Each winning attack can destroy up to 8% of the buildings in one random enemy province
Gold lootedNo gold taken

payments Loot

Trades destruction for profit — much lighter on the enemy's buildings, but every attack you win has a chance to steal some of their gold.

Province damageOnly 20% of normal. Each winning attack can destroy at most 3% of the buildings in one random enemy province (always at least 1 if the damage is big enough)
Gold lootedOnly on a winning ground attack (air and naval wins steal nothing): a random amount from 0 up to 10% of the defender's current gold (only 2% against bot nations)

info All war types, always

Regardless of war type, when a war ends because one side's morale hits 0, the winner takes 20% of every resource the loser has (30% with the Looting Teams tech). That end-of-war plunder is separate from the per-attack gold steal Loot-type wars get. Surrender and peace deals work differently (see How a War Ends).

rocket_launch Weapons of Mass Destruction (WMDs)

rocket Ballistic Missiles (ICBMs)

Long-range missiles that devastate enemy infrastructure and military units.

Cost$16M + 80 t aluminium + 50 t components + 15 t gasoline
Build LimitSilos + 1 (can build 1 without silos)
Launch RequirementAn active war and at least 1 ICBM in stock. No silo is needed to fire, silos only raise how many you can hold
TargetEnemy military units + random province damage. Iron Domes in the hit province can shoot down part of a salvo (up to half; more with the Star Wars Project tech)

Note: You need the ICBM Silo technology to buy ICBMs. With it you can hold 1 ICBM without any silos; silos let you store more and are needed to launch them in war.

dangerous Nuclear Weapons (Nukes)

The ultimate weapon. A nuke hits one province you choose and faces that province's Iron Domes. A single nuke is all-or-nothing: without the Star Wars Project tech it can't be shot down, with it the target province needs about 6–10 Iron Domes (10 is the maximum) for a real chance (roughly 35% at 6, almost certain at 9–10). A shot-down nuke is still used up. You review the expected damage and cost, then confirm, before it launches.

Cost$80M + 120 t aluminium + 100 t components + 25 t gasoline + 20 t uranium
Build Limit1 per Silo (requires silos)
How to launchWars → Attack → Nukes, or "Plan nuclear strike" on the enemy's country page (needs an active war)
DeathsDepend on density: the blast covers ~1,413 km², hitting the cities first. Many cities on little land = heavy losses; big spread-out provinces lose only a small share
DamageCities and buildings destroyed in proportion to the area hit; happiness in that province drops by up to 30 points
FalloutRadiation also kills 0.5% of the population of every province of the target nation, however spread out it is
Volley limitEach side can launch only ONE nuke per war (a shot-down nuke counts). Launching needs an active war
Repeat strikesHitting the same province again within 72h does less damage (each recent strike halves it)
Influence costFirst strike: 50% of your influence, and further launches compound. Retaliation (they nuked you first in this war): 25%. The penalty fades over 7 days
RequirementAt least 1,000,000 influence to launch, unless retaliating. Requires the Nuclear Testing Facility tech

Note: You cannot build nukes without silos. Each silo allows you to store 1 nuke.

construction Building Silos

Build Cost$350M + 1.08 kt steel + 480 t aluminium

Provides+1 ICBM capacity, +1 Nuke capacity (silos can be bombed and destroyed in war)

tips_and_updates WMD Technologies

Researched on the Upgrades page (cost in components). Chain: Better Engineering, Nuclear Testing Facility, Ballistic Missile Silo, ICBM Silo. Nuclear Testing Facility and ICBM Silo also need at least 2 Universities in your nation.

Ballistic Missile Silo40 t components - Prerequisite for the ICBM Silo
ICBM Silo50 t components - Required before you can buy ICBMs
Nuclear Testing Facility60 t components - Required before building nukes
Star Wars Project1,200 t components - Iron Domes intercept 25% more (also needs 2 Universities)

warning How to Use WMDs in War

  1. Declare war on an enemy nation
  2. Go to the Wars page and select the active war
  3. Click "Attack" to enter combat
  4. Select "Special Attack" (missiles/nukes option)
  5. Choose your WMD and target enemy units
  6. ICBMs you fire are used up and damage a random enemy province. Nukes open the nuclear strike planner instead, where you pick the province
sports_mma Combat System

psychology Morale

Morale determines your nation's will to fight.

Starting MoraleEach side starts a war at 100
Losing BattlesOnly the side that gets attacked and loses loses morale: roughly 5, 10 or 15 per lost battle (close, definite victory or annihilation). A failed attack costs the attacker 0 morale, so only defenders can be broken this way
0% MoraleThe war ends at once and the winner takes the war spoils
RecoveryMorale does not regenerate during a war

inventory Attack Costs

Attacks cost action points (AP) only. They do not use up ammunition or oil. Ground, air and naval each have their own AP pool: it holds up to 12 and refills by 1 every hour.

Ground Attack3 ground AP
Air Strike3 air AP
Naval Attack3 naval AP
ICBMs / NukesNo AP, but the missiles are used up

Daily plunderAfter a war ended by morale break or surrender, the winner takes a further 20% of every one of the loser's resources each day for 7 days (5% if the war was Raze). Wars ended by an agreed peace deal don't do this

tips_and_updates War Tips

  • Save action points for the branch (ground, air, naval) you need
  • Scout enemy military strength with spies first
  • Coordinate with coalition allies
  • Diversify your military composition
  • Don't overextend - watch your morale
  • Build silos before investing in WMDs
handshake How a War Ends

schedule Timing

Minimum lengthA war must run 24 hours before it can end by peace deal or surrender. Only a morale break (0 morale) can end it sooner
Auto-endA war nobody ends is closed automatically after 7 days

mail Peace Offer

Either side can propose peace and name resources the other side must hand over. The war ends when the other side accepts, and only the agreed terms change hands.

flag Surrender

A surrender needs both sides: you offer it, your enemy accepts or rejects it. If rejected, the war goes on.

ConditionalThe war ends and the winner takes 10% of every resource. No further penalty
UnconditionalThe winner takes the full spoils (20%, or 30% with Looting Teams) and your military size cap is halved for 5 days

diversity_3 Allies (Mutual Defense)

When you are attacked, your Mutual Defense partners can join the war against the aggressor as a counter-attack. The aggressor stays the aggressor.

JoiningThe ally must be within war range of the aggressor, not have ended another war in the last 3 days, and not have a Non-Aggression Pact with the aggressor
shield Defense System

security Defense

Attacking costs action points only. Defending is free: you automatically field all your units of the attacked domain.

  • Reduce incoming damage and protect your resources
  • Field units: your whole standing army of the attacked branch defends (ground: soldiers, tanks, artillery; air: fighters, bombers, apaches; naval: destroyers, cruisers, submarines)
  • Citizen Army: If you have no ground units left when a ground attack hits, a civilian militia fights instead. It can't win, but kills about 4–25% of every unit type the attacker sent (typically 10–20%, more for bigger nations). Air and naval attacks don't face it

edit No Defense Setup Needed

There is nothing to organize: defense is automatic. Every unit you own in the attacked branch fights, so the best defense is a large, balanced army in all three branches.

EntrenchmentGround units dug in defend up to +30% stronger (+10% per level, max level 3)
