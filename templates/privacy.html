privacy_tip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-15

This explains what Affairs and Order actually collects and why, in plain language. This is a small independent project, not a company with a legal department — if anything here is unclear, ask in the Discord.

What we collect

  • Account info — your username and email address, and a securely hashed version of your password (we never store or see your actual password).
  • Discord account link — if you connect Discord, we store your Discord user ID so bot commands and role syncing can find your nation. We don't receive your Discord password or messages.
  • Gameplay data — your nation's stats, resources, buildings, military, province ownership, coalition membership, and market/trade activity. This is the actual game.
  • Session cookie — a cookie that keeps you logged in. It's required for the site to work; it isn't used for tracking or advertising.
  • Activity timestamp — when you last used the game, so we can show accurate online/activity numbers and detect abandoned accounts.
  • Support (Patreon) — if you support the game on Patreon, we can see your patron status and pledge amount through Patreon's API to grant in-game perks. We never see or store your card/payment details — that's handled entirely by Patreon.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use third-party advertising trackers.
  • We don't share your email or Discord ID with other players.

Why we collect it

To run your account and let you play the game (contract necessity), to keep the game secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interest), and — for anything genuinely optional, like Discord linking or Patreon — because you chose to connect it (consent, which you can withdraw any time).

Your data, your control

You can permanently delete your account and its data at any time from your account settings — this removes your nation and account record. If you'd rather just export or check what we hold on you first, or have any other request about your data, reach out in the Discord and we'll sort it out directly.

Where data lives

Game data is hosted with our infrastructure provider (Railway). Discord linking uses Discord's own systems; Patreon support uses Patreon's own systems — both are governed by their own privacy policies for the data they hold directly.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll announce it in Discord. Continuing to play after a change means you've accepted the update.

See also: Terms of Service.
