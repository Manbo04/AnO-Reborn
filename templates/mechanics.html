domain Max Population

Your maximum population is determined by cities, land, and happiness. Cities and land give diminishing returns at very high counts instead of scaling forever.

Base Maximum1,000,000
Per City~+750,000 early on, tapering off at large counts
Per Land Slot~+120,000 early on, tapering off at large counts
Happiness Bonus+0.08% per % above 50
Pollution Penalty-0.04% per % above 50

family_restroom Demographics

Your population is divided into three age groups, each with unique effects.

ChildrenDo not work, consume 1.2x consumer goods, but will eventually age into workers.
Working AgeProvide the workforce for factories, mines, and military recruits.
ElderlyRetired citizens. Produce less tax but consume 2x consumer goods due to healthcare needs.

Buildings require power to operate efficiently. Without it, your economy suffers.

Power PlantsCoal Power Plants burn Coal. Nuclear Plants burn Uranium.
Energy ConsumptionIndustrial buildings and infrastructure consume MW (Megawatts) every hour.
Power Shortage-15% tax income and severely reduced factory output if you lack sufficient power.

warning Natural Disasters

Each hour, every nation has a small chance of a biome-appropriate disaster damaging a resource stockpile — reported in your Recent Activity feed.

Chance~0.1% per nation per hour (roughly once every 40 days)
EffectDestroys 10% of one stockpiled resource, capped at 500,000 kg per event

settings Advanced Game Systems

warning CRITICAL: Distribution System

You must have Distribution Centers to use Rations and Consumer Goods.

Even if you produce 1 million kg of rations, NOT A SINGLE CITIZEN WILL CONSUME THEM without distribution infrastructure.

Distribution CapacityTiered per building type — Food Banks 250k pop, Gas Stations 1.0M, Distribution Centers 1.5M, Farmers Markets 2.0M, General Stores 2.5M, Malls 5.0M
Rations BuildingsFood Banks, Distribution Centers, Gas Stations, General Stores, Farmers Markets, Malls
Consumer Goods BuildingsFood Banks, Distribution Centers, Gas Stations, General Stores, Malls
Effect of Missing DistributionPopulation doesn't grow, happiness drops, taxes plummet
Rule of ThumbBuild distribution infrastructure FIRST, then production

factory Chernobyl Rule

Production efficiency is limited by available workforce education.

FormulaEfficiency = jobs_available ÷ jobs_needed
Minimum20% efficiency floor (even if severely understaffed)
Affected BySchools train workforce for industrial buildings

local_shipping Distribution Bottleneck

CRITICAL: Consumer goods and rations require distribution buildings to reach citizens. Without distribution infrastructure, your resources are USELESS.

RequirementCapacity scales with building type (see table above) — cheap buildings like Food Banks serve less, Malls serve the most
Consumer Goods DistributionFood Banks, Distribution Centers, Gas Stations, General Stores, Malls
Rations DistributionFood Banks, Distribution Centers, Gas Stations, General Stores, Farmers Markets, Malls
Without DistributionRations & CG are hoarded, not consumed → -30% taxes, no growth, -5 happiness
Impact on EconomyMissing distribution causes productivity collapse and economic shutdown
