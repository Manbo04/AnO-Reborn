Combat Mechanics

Branch Action Points (AP)

Ground, air, and naval attacks cost 3 AP of their respective branch (start 6, max 12, +1/hour). Drone strikes cost 2 air AP, cruise missiles cost 2 naval AP.

Entrenchment (Dig In)

Dig In costs 2 ground AP (+1 level, max 3). Each level grants +10% ground defensive strength. Launching a ground attack resets entrenchment to 0.

War Intel & Recon

Recon Flights cost 2 air AP (+25 intel). Successful spy missions grant +20 intel. At 50+ intel enemy unit counts are revealed. Attacks gain up to +10% strength (+intel/10 %) and consume 25 intel.

Naval Blockade

Decisive naval victories (definite victory or annihilation) blockade the loser's ports for 24h, blocking market offers and trades. Winning any naval battle lifts the blockade early.
