Combat Mechanics

How Morale Loss Is Calculated

Morale loss depends on how decisive the win was and how big the winning force was (up to a full-strength attack), not on how large either army is. A close win costs the loser about 12 morale, a rout up to 40, so wars last several battles.

Damage & Loot

Casualties are applied proportionally to the units involved. If the war type is Loot and the attacker wins, they may seize up to 10% of the defender's gold.
