help_outlineHow the exchange works

Every price is in gold per unit, with no fee. Minting costs the issuer {{ currency_status.rate }} gold per unit, and only the issuer can turn it back into gold; everyone else trades it here or spends it on resource offers priced in it. Posting an offer locks what you offer (gold for a buy, the currency for a sell) until it's filled or cancelled. A nation behind on a defaulted bond can't have its currency trade above a cap (4 gold or lower) until the debt is paid.
