help_outline How it works

  • Every nation can mint its own currency at the Central Bank ({{ currency_status.rate }} gold = 1). Only the nation that issued a currency can turn it back into gold at that rate. Everyone else can trade it here or spend it on resource offers priced in it.
  • Posting an offer locks what you're offering right away: gold for a buy offer, the currency for a sell offer. Cancelling gives back whatever is left.
  • If a nation is behind on a defaulted bond, its currency can't trade above a price cap (4 gold per unit or lower, depending on how much it owes) until that debt is paid off.
