help_outlineHow auto orders work

An auto order keeps one normal market offer for a resource. Once an hour the game adds to it, but only as much as every limit you set allows:

  • Maximum on offer: never more than this many units listed at once.
  • Maximum per 24 hours: never add more than this in any 24 hours, so a sudden war can't strip your stockpile before you react.
  • Reserve: when selling, always keep at least this much. When buying, stop once you hold this much.

Example: sell iron at 236 gold, at most 500 on offer, at most 500 per day. If 380 is still listed and 120 was added in the last 24h, the next check adds 120. If any limit is reached, nothing is added that hour.

The offer works exactly like one you post yourself: resources (or the gold/currency for buy offers) are set aside when they're listed, anyone can fill it, and deleting the rule returns whatever is still listed. Buyers pay the {{ trade_fee_percent }}% transport fee as usual.
