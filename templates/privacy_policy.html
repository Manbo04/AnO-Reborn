privacy_tip Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Affairs and Order ("the game", "we", "us") is a browser-based nation-building game. This page explains what information we collect when you play, why, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, reach out on our Discord.

badge Account information

When you create an account, we store:

Nation name & biomeThe country name and starting biome you choose at signup.
Email addressUsed for login, password resets, and account security alerts (e.g. sign-ins from a new location).
PasswordStored as a salted bcrypt hash — we never store or can see your plain-text password.
Discord account (optional)If you sign up or log in with Discord, we receive your Discord user ID, username, and email address (scopes: identify, email).
Google account (optional)If you sign up or log in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address, and basic profile info (scopes: openid, email, profile).
IP addressLogged with sign-in attempts for fraud/abuse prevention and to detect logins from a new device or location.
sports_esports Gameplay data

Everything you do inside the game — your nation's stats, provinces, military, trades, coalition membership, chat messages, forum posts, and similar gameplay activity — is stored so the game can function, and is visible to other players where the feature is inherently public (e.g. rankings, the market, your nation page).

cookie Cookies
Session cookieStrictly necessary — keeps you logged in. Always set; the game cannot function without it.
Google AnalyticsSets cookies to measure site usage (pages visited, rough location, device type). Only loaded if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner.
Google AdSenseShown only to logged-out visitors, to display ads that help fund the game. Only loaded if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you reject cookies, logged-out visitors may still see non-personalized ads served without tracking cookies, or no ads at all.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, which will show the cookie banner again on your next visit.

groups Third parties we use
DiscordOptional login provider, and our community server/support channel.
GoogleOptional login provider (Google Sign-In), and Google Analytics/AdSense as described above.
PatreonOur store's "Buy Gems" option links out to our Patreon page to purchase a membership tier. We don't process payments ourselves or receive your payment details — Patreon handles that, and grants gems to your linked account based on your active tier.
RailwayOur hosting provider, which stores our database and server logs.
delete_forever Your rights & data deletion

Wherever you're located, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To request account deletion or a copy of your data, message us on Discord from an account you can verify is yours. We'll respond and action reasonable requests within 30 days.

child_care Children's privacy

Affairs and Order is not directed at children under 13, and you must confirm you meet the minimum age in our Terms of Service to create an account. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us on Discord and we'll remove it.

update Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. Material changes will be announced on our Discord. Continuing to use Affairs and Order after an update means you accept the revised policy.
