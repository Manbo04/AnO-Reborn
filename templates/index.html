Why we are building this

A nation simulator
you can learn from.

Affairs & Order is a game first, and we want it to be a serious simulation too: one where you can see why an economy grows, a population shrinks or an alliance breaks.

Systems that depend on each other

Population, production, trade, treaties and war all feed into one another on an hourly tick, so every decision has consequences you can trace.

Useful in the classroom

We are aiming for a simulation that economics, politics and international relations courses can use alongside a game anyone can enjoy.

Real players, not scripts

Markets, coalitions and wars are driven by people, and balance changes go to a community vote.

Early alpha
Playable today, still being built. Expect rough edges and changing numbers. Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us on Discord.
