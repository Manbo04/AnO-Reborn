help_outline How it works

  1. You propose a swap to another nation: what you give, what you get back (any resource or gold), how often, and optionally how many times.
  2. They accept or decline. Nothing moves until they accept — then the first swap happens straight away.
  3. It repeats automatically at the interval you picked (hourly up to weekly). Swaps are checked every 15 minutes, so one can land a few minutes late.
  4. It ends when it hits the max number of trades, or as soon as either side cancels.

infoNot enough resources? If either side can't cover their half when a swap is due, nothing moves and the agreement pauses. Either nation can resume it once both have enough. No fees — both sides get the full amounts.
