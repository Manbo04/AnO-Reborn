# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not post exploits publicly** (issues, public Discord channels, screenshots of live exploits).

Report privately by either:

- messaging a moderator or developer on [Discord](https://discord.gg/SEae3Ua), or
- using GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button under this repo's *Security* tab.

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and what an attacker could gain.

## What counts

Money or resource duplication, login or 2FA bypass, access to other players' data, injection, and anything that lets a player act as another player.

## Rewards

Valid private reports are paid **500M to 1B in-game gold**, plus fix bonuses, under the [Bug Bounty](BOUNTIES.md). If you used the bug for gain before reporting it, the report is not eligible.
